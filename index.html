Do I need to replace my current booking tool?

No. The reset starts with the WhatsApp and booking steps you already use. If a later tool change is worthwhile, you will know exactly why before spending on it.

What actually gets installed in 48 hours?

A native WhatsApp Business conversion layer: a greeting and away-message path, quick replies, six booking-stage labels, a lightweight quote/request form linked to a sheet, and a daily next-day confirmation routine. Your team keeps control of the number and the customer conversations.

No. The pilot does not promise a chatbot, WhatsApp API integration, or a new CRM. Those are separate projects only after the basic path proves it earns its keep.

How long does it take?

The pilot is designed to be installed in 48 hours once we have your services, service areas, working hours, and the person who manages customer messages.

What happens after the pilot?

You own the messages, intake structure, and team process. The seven-day follow-up window is there to fix small issues after your staff begins using it.
